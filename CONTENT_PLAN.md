# Luvia blog: content plan

Working list of posts to write, in priority order. Every post ships in EN + ES, with the usual
`BlogPosting` + `BreadcrumbList` schema, a `Sources` section where research exists, the
"not treatment" notice band, and a closing strip that links the matching Luvia feature.
Tone: quiet, evidence-led, no hype; say plainly when the evidence is modest.

Legend: **Hook** = the Luvia feature the post lands on. **KW** = primary search phrases.

---

## Series A: WOOP, one step at a time

The existing WOOP post is the hub; these are the spokes. Each is ~900–1200 words, one concrete
example carried through all four steps, and ends inside the WOOP screen in Rituals.

|---|------|-------|----|

## Series B: Meditation basics, honestly

| # | Post | Angle | KW |
|---|------|-------|----|
| B1 | **How to meditate for the first time (a 5-minute version)** | Sit, timer, one anchor, expect wandering, come back. No lineage talk. Ten minutes a day has RCT evidence (Harvard Health / Br J Health Psychol 2024; JMIR 2023). | how to meditate for beginners, 5 minute meditation |
| B2 | **When focusing on the breath makes it worse** | The core "techniques when breathing doesn't help" post. For anxiety, asthma, trauma, the breath is not a neutral anchor (Treleaven, trauma-sensitive mindfulness; Kristy Arbon's alternative anchors). Offer alternatives: feet/seat contact, sound, hands, counting, mantra, eyes-open soft gaze, walking. | meditation breath anxiety worse, meditate without focusing on breath |
| B3 | **Counting meditation: the technique you can't get wrong** | Counting breaths 1–10 and restarting; counting backwards from 20 (exhale only); visualising each number drawn slowly; what to do when you lose count (that *is* the practice). | counting meditation, count backwards meditation |
| B4 | **Noting: a word for every wandering** | Mahasi-style mental noting ("thinking", "planning", "itch"); ties to affect-labelling research (Torre & Lieberman 2018) so it also supports the mood tracker. | noting meditation technique, name it to tame it |
| B5 | **Body scan in 8 minutes, no audio** | Feet-up sequence you can do with a silent timer; brief body scans reduce stress in studies; good for people who fall asleep in sitting practice. | body scan meditation script, short body scan |
| B6 | **Mantra and phrase meditation for restless minds** | A silent syllable or short phrase on the out-breath; why a verbal anchor beats a sensory one for some people; loving-kindness phrases as a variant. | mantra meditation beginners, loving kindness phrases |
| B7 | **Open awareness: when you're ready to drop the anchor** | Choiceless awareness; why it is not a day-one practice; how to know you're ready. | open awareness meditation, choiceless awareness |
| B8 | **Visualisation meditation that doesn't turn into daydreaming** | Single static image (a candle, a number, a colour) held steady; contrast with outcome-fantasising, which links back to the WOOP post. | visualization meditation techniques |
| B9 | **Walking and eyes-open meditation** | For people who cannot sit still or feel unsafe with eyes closed; pace, gaze, feet as anchor. | walking meditation how to, eyes open meditation |
| B10 | **Meditation timer settings that actually matter** | Bells, length, interval bells, why streaks hurt; the case for a timer with nothing else on it. | meditation timer app, best meditation timer |
| B12 | **Meditation for focus: what ten minutes a day does to attention** ✅ written | Focused attention as attention training; Zeidan, Mrazek, Basso; honest about modest effects. | meditation for focus, does meditation improve attention |
| B11 | **Meditation side effects nobody mentions** | Relaxation-induced anxiety, unpleasant experiences in a minority of meditators (Britton lab); when to stop and talk to someone. Builds trust. | meditation makes me anxious, negative effects meditation |

Hook: Rituals → Meditation timer. B2–B9 are each "a technique you can run on Luvia's silent
timer with the bells off". B4 also links to the mood check-in.
