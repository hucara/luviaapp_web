features

Daily care, softly organized.

Luvia is an iPhone and iPad app for noticing the day without turning it into a performance. Mood, journaling, gratitude, breathing, calendar patterns, and anonymous kindness live in one calm place.

Not a substitute for mental health support. Luvia is not therapy, medical care, crisis support, or emergency care.

check in

Name the mood. Notice the pattern.

Choose a mood, add simple tags, and let the week chart show small shifts over time. The point is not scoring yourself. It is seeing the weather inside with a little more kindness.

what you can do

A private set of small rituals.

Journal without performing

Write a few words for the day, save a gratitude, and attach a photo when an image says enough.

Your data stays on your device

Private journal entries, gratitude notes, mood logs, breathe sessions, and settings are stored locally on your iPhone or iPad.

Breathe back into the day

Use a simple session timer with optional bells when you want a quieter minute instead of another feed.

See your inner weather

The calendar gathers moods, notes, gratitude, stillness, and kind messages into one soft month view.

one app, one gentle rhythm

Reflect, breathe, and return later.

Luvia keeps common self-reflection tools close together so you do not need a dashboard full of pressure. Open the app, write one honest line, start a breathing session, or check the month when you are ready.
