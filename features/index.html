features

Five things. One calm place.

Mood tracking, journaling, gratitude, a meditation clock, your data on your device — and one star feature built around kindness.

Not a substitute for mental health support. Luvia is not therapy, medical care, crisis support, or emergency care.

mood tracking

Name the mood. Notice the pattern.

Choose a mood, add simple tags, and let the week chart show small shifts over time. The point is not scoring yourself — it is seeing your inner weather with a little more kindness.

A monthly calendar gathers moods, notes, gratitude, sessions, and kind messages into one soft summary so patterns become visible without pressure.

journal & gratitude

Write the small true thing.

Add a few honest words for the day and save a gratitude note when something is worth holding. No audience, no streaks, no prompts telling you how to feel.

Attach a photo when an image says enough. Everything stays private on your device.

meditation clock

Breathe back into the day.

Set a session length and let the simple timer do the rest. Optional bells mark the start and end so you can close your eyes fully. No guided voice, no score — just a quiet minute when you need one.

data privacy

Your data stays on your device.

Stored locally

Journal entries, mood logs, gratitude notes, and breathing sessions live on your iPhone or iPad. Luvia does not upload your private reflections to a personal cloud account.

Download your data

Export everything you have written whenever you want. Your reflections belong to you and you can take them with you at any time.

App lock support

Use Face ID, Touch ID, or your device passcode to help keep the space protected from anyone who picks up your phone.

the star feature

Kind messages, without names.

Send something you are carrying, or sit with someone else's note when you need it. Anonymous, moderated, and shaped around care — not engagement.

important notice

Luvia is not medical or crisis support.

If you may hurt yourself or someone else, contact local emergency services or a trusted person immediately. Luvia is designed for reflection and gentle daily support, not diagnosis, treatment, or emergency help.
