Not a substitute for mental health support. Luvia is not therapy, medical care, crisis support, or emergency care.

private by default

Your reflections stay on your device.

Your journal entries, gratitude notes, mood logs, breathe sessions, and local settings are stored on your iPhone or iPad. Luvia does not upload private reflections to a personal cloud account.

Local-first journaling

Write the small true thing, save a gratitude, and keep it close.

App lock support

Use Face ID, Touch ID, or your device passcode to help protect the space.

anonymous care

Kind messages, without names.

Kind Messages are anonymous exchanges for supportive, non-romantic encouragement. Send what you carry, or sit with someone else's note when you can.

Safety checks before sharing

Luvia checks kind messages for contact details, threats, harassment, sexual content, spam, and other unsafe material.

Quiet breathing sessions

When words are too much, use the simple timer to breathe back into the day.
