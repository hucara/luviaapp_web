Luvia — Privacy Policy and Terms of Service

Last updated: May 21, 2026

This page covers both Luvia's Privacy Policy and Terms of Service. By using Luvia, you agree to both.

Contact: support@luviaapp.net

Privacy Policy

What stays on your device

Your journal entries, gratitude notes, mood logs, breathe sessions, and local settings are stored on your device. Luvia does not upload these private reflections to a personal cloud account.

Kind Messages

Kind Messages are anonymous exchanges between adult users. When you send, answer, report, or delete kind message data, limited message content and safety metadata may be processed by Luvia services so the feature can work and abuse can be prevented.

We process:

  • Message and reply text (so it can be moderated and delivered to another user)
  • A pseudonymous user identifier derived from Sign in with Apple
  • Locale and time-zone hints sent by the app
  • Safety state such as report counts, dismissals, and ban status
  • Crash and error telemetry (no journal, mood, gratitude, or breathe content)

We do not link messages to your real name, email address, contact list, or any third-party advertising identifier.

Sign in with Apple

Sign in with Apple is used for account access and Kind Messages abuse prevention. Luvia does not request your name or email address, and your Apple identifier is stored only as needed to protect the service.

Subscriptions

Subscription billing is handled by Apple and by our subscription provider, RevenueCat. Luvia receives entitlement status (active / not active) but does not receive your payment card or full billing details. RevenueCat may receive a billing email address provided by Apple in order to send subscription receipts.

Safety checks and moderation

Luvia runs local and server-side checks (including third-party moderation services such as OpenAI's moderation endpoint) to detect threats, harassment, sexual content, contact details, spam, or other unsafe content before a kind message is shared. Content that fails these checks may be blocked, hidden, or queued for human review.

Children and minors

Luvia is not intended for children or minors under 18. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under 18.

If you are under 18, do not use Luvia or submit personal information, journal content, Kind Messages, reports, or any other content through the app.

If we learn that we have collected personal information from someone under 18, we will delete it where reasonably possible, except where limited records must be retained for safety, abuse prevention, legal compliance, or protection of other users.

Your rights (GDPR / UK GDPR)

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, or another region with similar protections, you have rights to access, correct, delete, restrict, object to, or port the personal data we hold about you. Luvia services are operated from the EU. To exercise these rights, or to ask about the lawful basis for any processing, contact support@luviaapp.net. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority.

Your rights (California / US state privacy laws)

If you are a California resident or live in a US state with similar privacy laws, you have the right to request access to or deletion of personal information we hold about you, and to not be discriminated against for exercising those rights. Contact support@luviaapp.net to make a request.

Data retention

Local data lives on your device until you delete it or uninstall the app. Kind Messages content is retained only as long as needed to operate the feature, moderate it, and respond to reports. Banned users' minimal safety records may be retained longer to prevent re-abuse.

Backups and deletion

Device backups can include your journal, gratitudes, moods, and breathe sessions when you choose to export them. Kind Messages are excluded from backups. You can delete local kind message history from settings, and Luvia will request deletion of related server-side data where available. Account and Kind Messages data deletion remains available even if your subscription has ended.

What we do not do

Luvia does not sell your personal data. Luvia does not use your private journal, mood, gratitude, or breathe content for advertising. Luvia does not track you across apps or websites owned by other companies.

Third-party services

To operate Luvia we use:

  • Apple — Sign in with Apple, App Store billing, push and background delivery.
  • Supabase — hosting of Kind Messages backend and authentication, operated in the EU.
  • RevenueCat — subscription entitlement management.
  • OpenAI — automated moderation of Kind Messages content.
  • Resend — sending transactional billing emails.

Each of these processes only the minimum data needed for its function.

Changes

We may update this policy. Material changes will be reflected here with an updated "Last updated" date.

Contact

For privacy questions or deletion requests, contact support@luviaapp.net.
