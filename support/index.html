support

We're here to help.

Questions, feedback, a bug, or a request about your account? Reach out and a real person will get back to you.

Email: support@luviaapp.net

We aim to reply within 2 business days. Reports about Kind Messages are reviewed within 24 hours.

Common topics

Account & data deletion

You can delete your local data and your Kind Messages data from inside the app at any time. If you'd like help deleting your account or the email address associated with Sign in with Apple, email support@luviaapp.net and we'll take care of it.

Subscriptions & billing

Luvia Pro is billed through the Apple App Store. You can manage or cancel your subscription from your Apple ID settings. Refunds are handled by Apple according to App Store policy. If something looks wrong with your entitlement, contact us and we'll help sort it out.

Kind Messages & safety

Use the Report and Block actions inside the app to flag a message or stop contact from another user. We review reports within 24 hours. If you'd like to report something outside the app, email support@luviaapp.net.

Bugs & feedback

Found something that isn't working, or have an idea to make Luvia better? Tell us what happened, your device, and your iOS version if you can. Every message is read.

Privacy questions

For privacy requests or questions about how your data is handled, see our Privacy & Terms page or email support@luviaapp.net.

important notice

Luvia is not medical or crisis support.

If you may hurt yourself or someone else, contact local emergency services or a trusted person immediately. Support email is not monitored for emergencies.
